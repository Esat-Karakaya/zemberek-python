- NamedEntity is actually everything with an apostrophe
including:
    nouns with aphastrophies (kg'lik, Veli'yi)
    unkowns with aphastrophies (unkown'a)
    abbreviations (TÜİK'in)
    Foreign Words (link'e)
    Lowercase Letters (a'dan z'ye)
    words that need numbers (kullanıcı ismim sufx6'ydı)
but NOT:
    Completely unkown words (ni'ldu)
    hours and other numeric words (11:00'ın, 1.5'in, 5'in)
